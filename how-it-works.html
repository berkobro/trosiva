How it works

Watch it work.

Five bags. One disc. Joined by perforations so the next bag is ready the moment the last one comes out.

Step by step

Set it. Forget it.

01

Set

Place a Trosiva disc on top of your 13-gallon kitchen can. Five bags sit stacked and ready, connected by perforations.

02

Push It

Press the flat center down to open the first bag into the can.

03

Fill

Use your trash can like you always do.

04

Pull

When it's full, lift the bag out. It separates along the perforation, and the next bag stays right where it belongs.

05

Repeat

Set the next bag into the can and you're done. No reaching into the bin, no hunting for the box. Five bags later, drop in a fresh disc.

FAQ

Questions

What is a disc?

A disc is five Trosiva trash bags stacked together and joined by perforations. You set the whole disc on your can and use the bags one after another.

What size can does it fit?

Trosiva is made for standard 13-gallon kitchen trash cans.

How many bags do I get?

Every disc holds 5 bags. A 5-disc box is 25 bags, 10 discs is 50, 15 discs is 75 and 20 discs is 100.

Do I need a special trash can?

No. Trosiva works with the can you already have.
